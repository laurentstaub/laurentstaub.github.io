What I do

Operational finance and AI

One material — your sales, your costs, your contracts — rebuilt into a model that stays with you. Data tools and AI serve that production chain; they are not the deliverable.

  • Business plan and profit and loss

    Consolidate sales, rebuild margins, project five years out, and show what breaks first.

  • Costs and manufacturing capacity

    Turn a sales plan into machine hours, cost of goods and break-even by product.

  • Pricing and market access

    Price sensitivity, treated population, reimbursement and public procurement: what price does to volume, and the reverse.

  • Due diligence

    Rebuild the vendor's model instead of reading it, isolate break-even, list the questions left open. An example of this work →

Where I work

The pharmaceutical industry, and its international side

Long, regulated cycles, administered prices, negotiated market access, supply tensions, sensitive data. I know the sector from the inside, from manufacturing to the community pharmacy — where I still practise.

The plans I build rarely stop at one country: portfolios of licensing and supply agreements across some forty countries, manufacturing sites in Europe and India, pricing policy and public procurement in China. Geography is part of the model.
