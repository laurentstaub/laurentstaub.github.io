Our conviction

A plan is only as good as its assumptions

Every project and every business plan rests on assumptions — about markets, costs, regulatory timelines, pricing and reimbursement. Most remain implicit, and that is exactly where portfolios go off course. We put them at the center of the decision.

Surface

Write down, in black and white, the assumptions each project or plan depends on: what has to be true for this to work?

Test

Confront each assumption with the available data — yours and the market's — to separate what is proven from what is hoped.

Track

Follow assumptions over time the way you follow a budget: decisions to continue, pivot or stop become factual, not political.

Our services

Four levers for clear-eyed decisions

From choosing a project to delivering it, we step in where resource-allocation decisions are made.

Project portfolio management

Map your projects — launches, line extensions, industrialization — with their assumptions and interdependencies. Put governance and reviews in place that let you arbitrate continuously.

Project selection & investment decisions

Build explicit selection criteria, prepare business cases — new market, new line, new product — and compare options on a consistent basis, so your resources go where they pay off.

AI implementation

Identify the use cases that actually create value, get your data ready and deploy pragmatic AI solutions — from pilot to production, while meeting the sector's requirements: health data, quality, traceability.

Business planning

Model your results, financing needs, industrial load and your pricing and reimbursement scenarios. Business plans where every number traces back to an identified assumption — and can therefore be revised.

Our home turf

Rooted in the pharmaceutical industry and healthcare

We know the sector from the inside: long, regulated cycles, negotiated market access, administered prices, supply tensions, sensitive data. It is our home turf, and it forged our standards.

Those standards travel well: the discipline of selecting projects and tracking assumptions applies to any business that commits resources to uncertain bets.
