About

Laurent Staub

Doctorate in pharmacy. I build the business plans and cost models of pharmaceutical companies, and the data tools that feed them. Antheos Data is an independent SASU, based in Paris. One contact, no subcontracting.

The counter, the plant, the spreadsheet

I started in community pharmacy, and I still practise there. It is the only place where you see what an administered price, a supply disruption or a reimbursement decision actually produce, at the counter. That point of view is not obtained by reading a market report.

Since 2016 I have worked for pharmaceutical companies and contract manufacturers: product business plans, sales consolidation, capacity and cost of goods models, price sensitivity studies, acquisition files. Manufacturing sites in Europe and India, licence portfolios across some forty countries, pricing policy in China. The longest engagements are renewed year after year, which is the only measure of quality that interests me.

Data as a tool, not as decoration

My doctoral thesis in pharmacy deals with data visualisation in public health, based on drug shortages. It left me with one working conviction: a chart is not an illustration of a conclusion, it is an instrument of verification. When a curve is surprising, it is often the data that needs to be looked at again.

I develop what I model. The financial model and the data infrastructure that confronts it with reality come from the same hands: spreadsheets, project databases, dashboards, consolidation scripts, and machine learning where it saves time. That avoids the usual seam between the person who calculates and the person who codes.

What I publish

I publish two things here. Articles on durations, endpoints, counting conventions and the traps of analysis — the subjects where a methodological error is expensive in a valuation. And an observatory of verified, versioned and dated datasets, the first of which lists the advanced therapies authorised in the European Union.

Two tools are online and open to all. The drug shortage tracker follows supply tensions in France. Médipile makes the French public medicines database usable at the pharmacy counter: summaries of product characteristics and patient leaflets quoted section by section, interactions, dispensing conditions, and alternatives without a given excipient.

How I work

Blocks of a few weeks, working directly with the finance or development function. The output is a model you can open and a set of slides that accounts for it. My clients' data stays with them: nothing entrusted to me feeds a publication or work for a third party.

As a pharmacist, I am bound to present my activity factually: you will find no testimonial, no client logo and no superlative on this site. What remains is verifiable.

A plan to put to the test?

Write to me with what you have: a business plan, a budget, a memorandum. The first conversation is free of commitment.
